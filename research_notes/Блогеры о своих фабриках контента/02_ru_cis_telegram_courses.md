# RU/CIS Telegram channels, authors and schools selling AI "content factories" (контент-завод, нейроконтент, n8n, вайбкодинг)

Method note (important for the report writer): research was done on 2026-09-30. Direct page fetches were blocked by the egress proxy for vc.ru, habr.com, sostav.ru, cossa.ru, tgstat.ru, t.me, mayai.ru and neirocoder.ru. Every finding below therefore comes from **search-engine snippets or summaries** of those pages, not from full-page reads. Treat numbers as "snippet-level" and re-check them before quoting them as hard facts. Subscriber counts are point-in-time snapshots from telemetr/t.me previews as surfaced by search, and their exact dates are unknown. Nearly all revenue, student and result numbers are **self-reported by sellers** unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Which Telegram channels, authors and schools are most visible, what they sell, and at what price

### Takeaway
The RU "контент-завод" niche is fragmented. There is no single dominant channel. It is a mid-tier ecosystem of author-led Telegram channels with roughly 3k–20k subscribers, each feeding a paid club (about 5–7k ₽/month) or a course (about 4k–270k ₽). Big edtech players (Zerocoder, Skillbox, Netology, Brunoyam, Stepik authors) sell adjacent "n8n / ИИ-агенты" programs. The stack is now fairly standard: n8n or Make.com, plus OpenRouter/GPT/Claude, plus image and video generators, plus Google Sheets/Notion, with autoposting to Telegram, VK, MAX and Instagram. It is shifting toward "вайбкодинг" (Cursor, Claude Code) in 2026.

### Inferences
- Pricing ladder in this niche:
  - free Telegram channel
  - low-ticket mini-course or templates (4–20k ₽)
  - monthly club (5.8–6.8k ₽)
  - flagship cohort (50–250k ₽)
  - "strategy with the author" or done-for-you (270–500k ₽)
- The "контент-завод" label has been taken up both by genuine automation teachers (Make.com / n8n builders) and by mass-market "earn on AI" info-business sellers who previously sold marketplace courses (e.g. Палаш). Treat these as two distinct segments.
- 2026 trend: rebranding from n8n/Make toward "вайбкодинг" (Cursor, Claude Code). Examples: Хорошев's club renamed to "Вайбкодинг и ИИ-агенты"; Product University moved its factory program to Claude Code.
- Channel sizes in the builder segment are modest (about 3–20K). The largest RU AI channels are news or aggregator channels, not build-log channels.

## 3. How creators talk: typical claims, framing, red flags, audience and critic reactions

### Takeaway
Sales framing leans hard on "autopilot / without a team / without experience / pays for itself". Critics on Habr, and even agency bloggers on sostav, push back: no working content factory runs without humans, cheap offers hide real costs, and some courses are "ChatGPT + a template + manual uploading". Heavy piracy of courses on "складчина" sites is a sign of both demand and low perceived value.

### Cited Findings
- Habr, "Контент-завод в 2026 году: разбор автономных систем, который отговорит вас это покупать" (tagged "ai slop"):
  - Main claim: **no working content factory today operates without human involvement.**
  - It analyzes an "AI content factory" program promising **10,000–50,000 ₽/day with no experience**. As of **February 2026** that program had **zero positive reviews**, and buyers called the training useless and the author a scammer.
  - Inside, it found "ChatGPT + a VideoLeap template + manual upload to TikTok", i.e. the client does the work.
  - It also discusses Jasper and "turnkey content factories for marketplaces".

  [habr.com/ru/articles/1039432](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1039432/); [braintools mirror](https://www.braintools.ru/article/30771)
- sostav.ru columnist: "Контент-завод: сколько это реально стоит и почему вам врут про "дёшево"". Realistic minimum is 130–150k ₽/month. The same author also writes "Контент-завод, SMM или нейросети: почему рынок массово путает эти понятия" — [sostav 77572](https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/283819/77572); [sostav 89630](https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/283819/89630)

### Inferences
- Red-flag pattern:
  - income-per-day promises
  - "no experience needed"
  - fully autonomous claims
  - screenshots instead of channel links
  - pivots from a previous "money" niche (marketplaces, crypto) into AI
  - flagship prices of 100k+ ₽ with little verifiable output
- Builder-style authors (Хорошев, Румянцева, Нейроцех) sell cheaper recurring clubs and templates. Their credibility rests on showing workflows, which the audience can check.
- The skeptical counter-narrative is now mainstream in RU tech media (Habr "ai slop" framing, sostav columnists), not only in fringe comments.

### Gaps
- I could not read Telegram comment threads or vc.ru comments (fetch blocked), so audience reactions come only from critical articles and review sites, not from first-hand comment samples.
